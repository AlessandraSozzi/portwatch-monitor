This is not an official estimate of the IMF. Rather, this page presents an experimental nowcasting model of maritime trade, leveraging real-time satellite data on vessel movements. The model aims to provide a timely indicator of merchandise trade. Exports and imports are both valued on a free-on-board (fob) basis. For the European Union and individual economies, only series approved for publication are shown. The estimates are updated on a monthly basis with a lag of 7 working days and are subject to weekly revisions. For background on the methodology, please see here. For limitations of the data and approach, please see here.
